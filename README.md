# Brownian Tensor Criticality

Exploratory mathematical research on eigenvector geometry and critical points of symmetric fourth-order tensors, including real homogeneous ternary quartics.

## Research tracks

The repository originated as an investigation of critical scaling, eigenvector instability, and universality for Brownian-evolving symmetric fourth-order tensors. A subsequent exploratory track, **BTC-004**, examined Morse-index realizability of real projective eigenpoints of ternary quartics.

## BTC-004: Real eigenpoints of ternary quartics

For a real homogeneous quartic \(F(x,y,z)\), eigenpoints are projective directions where \(\nabla F(v)\) is parallel to \(v\). Nondegenerate real eigenpoints are classified by the Morse index of \(F\) restricted to \(\mathbb{RP}^2\).

**Exploratory phase closed on 5 November 2026.**

- **Internal realization ledger:** 19 of 21 considered Morse triples have supporting evidence of varying verification strength. This is **not** a claim that all 19 have complete, independently reproduced exact certificates.
- **Unresolved triples:** \((6,6,1)\) and \((1,6,6)\). Their existence or impossibility has not been established.
- **No general obstruction proved:** The explored families do not establish a universal bound of five minima.
- **Novelty and publication status:** No confirmed novel theorem or discovery; novelty remains undetermined.
- **Five-point deformation table:** Historical reported numerical/algebraic summaries require preservation and independent checking of the underlying exact projective certificates before they can be treated as verified.

The investigation used Morse theory, algebraic eigenpoint equations, exact-symbolic reasoning, and bounded parameter explorations. The work is presented as an **exploratory research archive**, not as a completed classification theorem or peer-reviewed publication.
