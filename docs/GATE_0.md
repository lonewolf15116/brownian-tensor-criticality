# Gate 0 — Literature and mathematical feasibility

Status: OPEN / DOCUMENTATION ONLY (2026-11-02).

## Research question
Is a single minimum absolute tangent-Hessian eigenvalue sufficient to characterize local angular diffusion of a continuously tracked Z-eigenvector of a Brownian-evolving symmetric fourth-order tensor on R^4?

## Allowed
Literature search, verified bibliography, symbolic derivations, explicit counterexample construction on paper, and protocol drafting.

## Not authorized
Numerical simulations, random sweeps, theorem/novelty assertions, manuscript submission, or moving to Gate 1 without owner approval.

## Exit criteria
1. Verify nearest prior art with precise theorem-level comparisons.
2. Specify symmetric tensor Brownian covariance and normalization.
3. Distinguish eigenvector branch continuation from re-selection and sign equivalence x ~ -x.
4. Derive or refute the single-gap sufficiency claim with explicit assumptions.
5. Record counterexamples, unresolved issues and owner decision.

No discoveries confirmed. No experimental results.
