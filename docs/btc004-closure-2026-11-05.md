# BTC-004 — Research Closure and Roadmap Reconciliation

**Closure date:** 2026-11-05  
**Disposition:** CLOSED — exploratory phase completed; mathematical questions retained  
**Scope:** BTC-004 even ternary-quartic Morse-index realizability and associated exploratory constructions.

## Scientific status at closure

- **Realization ledger:** 19 of 21 topologically admissible Morse triples are internally supported, with varying certification strength. This is **not** a claim of 19 independently reproduced exact certificates.
- **Unresolved:** `(6,6,1)` and, by quartic negation, `(1,6,6)`.
- **No universal impossibility theorem:** Neither `m_0 <= 5` nor exclusion of the two missing triples has been proved.
- **Novelty:** No confirmed novel theorem or discovery; literature priority and publishability remain undetermined.
- **Research status:** The bounded exploratory program is closed. The mathematical question remains open.

## Bounded evidence and limitations

1. The baseline sheared affine quartic has a reported exact nine-real-critical-point calculation with affine Morse triple `(5,4,0)`; this is **not** the finite-`s` projective triple.
2. Five `s=100` projective samples were **reported** as:
   
   | (alpha, beta) | Reported real projective eigenpoints | Reported Morse triple |
   |---|---:|---|
   | (0, 0) | 11 | (5,5,1) |
   | (-2, -5) | 11 | (4,5,2) |
   | (-2, -20) | 9 | (3,4,2) |
   | (0, 2) | 7 | (3,3,1) |
   | (-20, -20) | 11 | (3,5,3) |

   **Evidence status:** These values are historical *reported outputs*, not independently verified certificates in this closure record. Their Euler consistency does not prove correctness. Exact elimination polynomials, complete chart coverage, isolated real roots, and certified Hessian signs must be located or reproduced before upgrading their status.
3. The `(alpha,beta)` scan is an exploratory negative: no target witness was reported. This does not prove a family-wide obstruction or monotonicity of the minimum count.
4. The four-variable `F(x,y,z)+mu*w^4` discussion is a separate exploratory direction, **not** a solution to the ternary problem. Mixed real lifts depend on signs of critical values; the baseline zero-eigenvalue point causes degeneracy without further treatment.
