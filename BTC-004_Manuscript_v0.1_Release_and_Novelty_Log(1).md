## Mathematical scope

For arbitrary real `d1,d2,d3,a,b,c`, the Morse index triples of 13 distinct real projective critical points of an even homogeneous ternary quartic are only `(4,6,3)` and `(3,6,4)`. Both are attained. This is a **restricted-family assertion**, not a global impossibility theorem for arbitrary ternary quartics. It also does not establish original novelty.
